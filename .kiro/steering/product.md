# Bunkar OS — Domain & UI

## Glossary
- **bazaar** — piece received back from the weaver (inbound event).
- **tauli** — weighment of yarn/piece; drives wastage calc.
- **peshgi** — advance paid to a weaver, carried as a ledger balance.
- **kharcha** — expenses/costs charged to a job or weaver.
- **roker** — owner's running ledger / cash+goods account view.
- **thekedar** — contractor/middleman who manages multiple weavers.
- **munshi** — clerk/accountant who operates the app day-to-day.
- **gaj** — unit of length = 36 inches.
- **girah** — 1/16 of a gaj.
- **KPSI** — knots per square inch (carpet density/quality).
- **lot / shade** — dye batch grouping; shade must match within a piece.
- **abrash** — visible shade variation across a carpet (defect/characteristic).

## UI constraints
- **Hindi / Hinglish** copy first.
- **Big numerals** — numbers are the primary content.
- Color code: **red = given**, **green = received**.
- Precision: **kg → 3 decimals**, **sq ft → 2 decimals**.
- **Offline-first** — works with no network; sync later.
- Target **low-end Android** — keep it light and fast.
- **Preview-and-confirm before any WhatsApp share** — never auto-send.
- **No SMS / contacts permissions.**
